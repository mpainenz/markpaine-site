# Asset licensing

The MIT license in this repository covers the source code and documentation.

`public/headshot.jpg` is a personal photograph of Mark Paine. It is included
only to demonstrate the live site and is not licensed for reuse,
redistribution, or use in derived sites.

Fork owners must replace that image with a photograph or illustration they
have permission to use. Update `portraitPath` in `data/site.mjs` if the
replacement has a different filename.

The historical WorldxChange emblem is from the [2007 Telecommunications Carriers’ Forum annual report, printed page 6](https://www.tcf.org.nz/wp-content/uploads/2007-TCF-Annual-Report.pdf). The [ihug wordmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:Logo_ihug_nz.png) identifies the former New Zealand ISP. Company marks belong to their respective owners.

The Sulaco Technology emblem is cropped from the [original logo on its archived website](https://web.archive.org/web/20150113104701id_/http://www.sulaco.co.nz/static/css/images/sulaco-logo.png?bc6276c4811b).

The Solus Designs mark, *Tukino Light – Indigo Dusk*, adapts the eastern skyline from [*Mt Ruapehu dressed In Snow* by Geoff McKay](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mt_Ruapehu_dressed_In_Snow.jpg), licensed under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). The illustration uses a simplified skyline and an indigo palette.
